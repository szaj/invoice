# Out of Scope

> [!abstract] Related
> [[Product Goals]] · [[Product Overview]] · [[00 Home]]

Version 1 explicitly excludes:

- Customer login portal or customer account dashboard
- General ledger / double-entry accounting
- Expense management and vendor bills
- Inventory / stock management
- Payroll
- Purchase orders
- Tax filing or statutory accounting submissions
- Automated debt collection beyond email reminders
- Native mobile apps

Customer-facing payment is **hosted checkout links only**, not a portal. See [[PDF and Email]] and [[Payments]].
